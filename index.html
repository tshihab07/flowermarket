Flower Care Guide

Essential Tips for Healthy Flowers

Simple steps to keep your flowers vibrant and long-lasting.

Proper Watering

Change water every 2-3 days and trim stems at a 45° angle to maximize hydration and prevent bacteria growth.

Sunlight Needs

Most flowers thrive in bright, indirect light. Avoid direct afternoon sun to prevent wilting and petal damage.

Soil & Nutrition

Use well-draining soil and add flower food to the water every few days to supply essential nutrients.
